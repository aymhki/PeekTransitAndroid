Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Peek Transit is a third-party application that utilizes the Winnipeg Transit API to provide bus stop information, schedules, and transit widgets. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Winnipeg Transit or The City of Winnipeg.


Data Usage & Attribution

All transit data is provided by permission of Winnipeg Transit, a department of The City of Winnipeg. The data is licensed under the Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL).


Privacy Policy

Peek Transit accesses location data to provide transit information but does not store this data permanently. Your favorite routes and stops are stored locally on your device. No analytics or tracking services are implemented. Location data is used solely to display relevant transit information and is not shared with third parties.

Data Collection and Usage

Location Data:
- Peek Transit accesses your device's location to provide nearby transit stops and real-time transit information
- Location data is used locally on your device to determine relevant transit routes and stops
- Location information is not stored on servers or shared with third parties
- You can control location access through your device settings

The following data may be accessed:
- Approximate location (network-based)
- Precise location (GPS and network-based) when available
- Background location (GPS and network-based) when using widgets
